Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

The short version: Popdraftz doesn't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. What the app remembers stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Popdraftz saves a few things on your device so the app works the way you left it:

  • the handle you type in, and your friend's name for same-device games
  • your last format, category and player pool
  • your win–loss record
  • whether you've seen the tutorial

This never leaves your phone, and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Playing a friend on another phone

When you create or join a room with a room code, the two phones exchange game messages through Supabase Realtime, a service that relays them between devices. Those messages contain your handle, your bids and picks, the state of the game, and a random ID for the session. They're passed along live and aren't stored; when the room closes, they're gone. Like any internet service, Supabase briefly handles technical information such as your IP address to make the connection. See the Supabase privacy policy.

Your handle is only shown to the friend you share your room code with. Don't use your real name if you'd rather not.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, email or password
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • No ads, analytics or tracking across apps and websites
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone

Children

Popdraftz is fine for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Photos in the app

Item photos come from Wikimedia Commons, Flickr and Pixabay under their licenses, and every one is credited inside the app (the camera button). If you're shown in a photo and want it removed, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
