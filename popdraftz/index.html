Support

Popdraftz is a 1v1 draft game. Items come up one at a time and you bid for them blind against a friend or popbot. Draft the better team in Classic, or draft a soccer XI or a football lineup and watch the game play out live.

Need help? Email mocomapshelp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I play a friend on another phone?

Both of you open Play a friend. One taps Create a room and shares the four-letter code; the other taps Join a room and types it in. Whoever creates the room picks the format and category. Both phones need an internet connection.

It says there's no room with that code

The room only exists while it's open on your friend's phone. Check the code (it never uses 0, O, 1, I or L) and make sure they haven't left the room screen.

How do I see the rules again?

Tap the ? in the top corner of the mode screen to replay the tutorial.

Where's my record saved?

On your phone. There are no accounts, so deleting the app resets it.

Who took the photos?

Every item photo is credited in the app: tap the camera button. To have a photo of you removed, email us.
